# Mishin Cu potential notice

`potentials/Cu01.eam.alloy.txt` contains the EAM potential developed by Yuri
Mishin, Michael J. Mehl, Dimitrios A. Papaconstantopoulos, Arthur F. Voter, and
Joel D. Kress:

Y. Mishin et al., "Structural stability and lattice defects in copper: Ab
initio, tight-binding, and embedded-atom calculations," *Physical Review B* 63
(2001) 224106, <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.63.224106>.

Original distributions:

- NIST Interatomic Potentials Repository:
  <https://www.ctcms.nist.gov/potentials/entry/2001--Mishin-Y-Mehl-M-J-Papaconstantopoulos-D-A-et-al--Cu-1/>
- OpenKIM model
  `EAM_Dynamo_MishinMehlPapaconstantopoulos_2001_Cu__MO_346334655118_006`:
  <https://doi.org/10.25950/7be9a87f>

The NIST-compatible SETFL conversion was produced by Chandler Becker on 4
February 2009 from files provided by Yuri Mishin. NIST requests that users
credit its repository in addition to citing the original paper.

The OpenKIM distribution identifies this model as CDDL-1.0 licensed. A copy of
that license is included as `Mishin-Cu-CDDL-1.0.txt`.

The file in this package is byte-for-byte identical to OpenKIM's
`Cu01.eam.alloy`; only its package filename has the additional `.txt` suffix.
Its SHA-256 checksum is
`5e374260448eea5c26de59432d75e702c1778cc6999d2527a789e5387ce9dc9e`.
